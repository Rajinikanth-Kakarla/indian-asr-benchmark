
# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We aim to make participation in Indian ASR Benchmark welcoming and respectful to everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Welcome different backgrounds, languages, and levels of experience.
- Provide useful feedback without personal attacks.
- Focus on improving the project and its technical quality.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, and sharing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainer through a contact method listed in the repository profile. Do not publish private or sensitive information in a public issue.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove inappropriate contributions and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
